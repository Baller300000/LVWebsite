A MAP IS A MODEL

Every map leaves something out.

Maps help us make decisions by selecting and arranging information, but the paper or pixels are not the ground. Scale, projection, survey date, symbols, accessibility detail, and purpose change what is visible. Use appropriate current mapping for travel, check official sources where precision matters, and treat historic or thematic maps as artifacts with context. These guides develop a careful reading habit; they are not turn-by-turn directions, navigation advice for hazardous terrain, or substitutes for local knowledge and safety equipment.
