Monembasia (For real now)

Okay, I have told you a lot about going to (and then fro).

Now, I am in Monembasia. The castle is beautiful
(Its Beau Tillman, for extreme OMITB fans), but at night you cannot see it because lights
for lighting up the castle are not installed. When I went on the peak of the castle, the view was
flabbergastingly beautiful! The fauna was lush green, and it filled every centimeter
(Inch or foot or oil per eagle if you are 'Murican) of the area. The sky was more blue than
I honestly do not know what. The only problem was that my knees were how do I put this?
completely unusable. After going down the castle's mountain, I think we went home? We had walked so much,
my memory was not functioning propperly, maybe did not function at all?
