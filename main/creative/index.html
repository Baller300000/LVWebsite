A PERMISSION SLIP

Your first draft is allowed to be strange.

Creative practice is not a mysterious talent switch. It is a sequence of noticing, selecting, arranging, testing, and revising. This workshop is full of practical prompts rather than promises of instant mastery. Work with materials you can access, check permissions before using other people's work or images, and ask for feedback that respects the creator and the audience. The pages are introductions, not universal laws; culture, purpose, disability, medium, and personal taste all matter. Change one thing at a time, save versions, and see what a deliberate decision does.
