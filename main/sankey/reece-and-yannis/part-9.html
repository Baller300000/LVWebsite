Yannis had spent years negotiating visibility. Too much, and he became a target. Too little, and he disappeared into something that wasn’t him. The balance was never stable. But with Reece, something unfamiliar happened. He didn’t feel like he was adjusting. He felt… observed. Fully. One evening, Yannis showed up wearing something he hadn’t worn outside his room before. It wasn’t dramatic. Not by most standards. But for him, it was exposure. A soft, fitted top. Subtle, but unmistakably shaped. Paired with loose layers that didn’t quite hide it. Reece noticed immediately. Of course he did. But he didn’t react. Not outwardly. They walked together in silence for a few minutes before Yannis spoke. “You’re very quiet today.” Reece glanced at him. “I’m always quiet.” “Not like this.” Reece hesitated. “I don’t want to say the wrong thing.” Yannis slowed slightly. “About what?” Reece exhaled. “You look… different.” Yannis smiled faintly. “That again.” Reece shook his head quickly. “No. I mean—” He stopped. Reset. “You look like yourself. Just… more.” That landed differently. Yannis felt it. “More what?” he asked. Reece met his gaze. “More visible.” They stopped walking. Not intentionally. It just happened. The space between them felt smaller. Not physically. But in everything else. “You’re not uncomfortable?” Yannis asked. There was no defensiveness in the question. Just quiet caution. Reece frowned slightly. “Why would I be?” Yannis held his gaze for a moment longer than usual. “Most people are.” Reece considered that. Then: “I think… I’m only uncomfortable when I don’t understand something.” A pause. “And I understand you more now.” Yannis looked away first. Not out of avoidance. Out of something softer.
