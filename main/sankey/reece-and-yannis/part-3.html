The first real break came from something small. It always does. Reece saw Yannis across campus, standing with a group he didn’t recognize. Louder people. Sharper voices. The kind that filled space aggressively instead of naturally. Yannis laughed at something one of them said. But it wasn’t the same laugh. Reece knew that immediately. He watched longer than he should have. One of them said something—too quiet to hear—but Yannis’s shoulders stiffened just slightly before he brushed it off. Reece looked away. He told himself it wasn’t his place. Later, when Yannis came to the fountain, something felt off. Reece could feel it in the way he sat. The way he didn’t quite settle. “You okay?” Reece asked. Yannis blinked, surprised. “Yeah. Why?” “You seem… different.” A pause. Then Yannis smiled. That same not-quite-right smile. “I’m always different.” Reece shook his head. “No. I mean—” He stopped. Words failed him. Yannis’s expression shifted again. Sharper now. “What do you mean?” Reece hesitated too long. “Nothing,” he said. That was the wrong answer. Yannis stood. “Right.” His voice wasn’t loud. It didn’t need to be. Reece felt the distance immediately. “I didn’t mean—” “I know,” Yannis cut in. But he didn’t stay.
