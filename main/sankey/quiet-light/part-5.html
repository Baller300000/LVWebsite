It rained the day they spoke again. Not heavy. Just enough to blur edges and empty the courtyard. Reece was still there. Of course he was. Alex stood at the edge for a long moment before walking over. “You’re going to ruin your sketchbook.” Reece looked up. There was no surprise in his expression. “You came back.” Alex exhaled. “I didn’t leave.” Reece nodded slightly. “Okay.” A pause. Then, carefully: “I’m sorry.” Alex watched him. “For what?” “For not saying what I meant.” “And what did you mean?” Reece closed the sketchbook. “I meant… you seemed like you were trying harder with them. Like you were… adjusting.” He hesitated. “I didn’t think that was fake. I just didn’t understand it.” Silence. Rain tapped softly against stone. Alex sat down. “That’s because you don’t have to do it,” he said. Reece frowned. “Do what?” “Translate yourself.” Alex looked at his hands. “Some people only accept you in certain formats.” Reece absorbed that. Slowly. “So you change?” Alex shook his head. “I adapt.” A beat. “Or I get rejected.” Reece felt something shift in his chest. Not sharp. Not sudden. Just… real. “I didn’t think about that,” he said. “I know.” Not accusatory. Just factual. They sat in silence again. But it wasn’t the same silence as before. This one had weight. And understanding.
