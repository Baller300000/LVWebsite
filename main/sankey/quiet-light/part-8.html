Alex never dressed the same way twice in the first weeks after they reconnected. Reece noticed. Not in the way others did—quick glances, surface-level judgments—but in the details. The intention behind it. The quiet decisions. Some days, Alex leaned into softness completely. Loose sweaters that slipped off one shoulder, thin rings that caught the light when he moved his hands, pale fabrics that blurred the lines of his frame instead of defining them. Other days, there was restraint. Simpler clothes. Neutral tones. Less visible effort. Reece didn’t comment on it. But he watched. And slowly, he understood. Alex caught him staring once. Not in a crowded space. Not when it could be dismissed. It was just the two of them, sitting on the library steps in the late evening, the campus quieter than usual. Reece’s gaze lingered too long on the way the sleeve of Alex’s sweater fell over his hand. Alex tilted his head. “You’re analyzing me again.” Reece blinked, pulled back slightly. “I wasn’t—” “You were.” Not accusatory. Just certain. Reece hesitated. Then, instead of retreating like he usually would, he stayed. “I’m trying to understand,” he said. Alex’s expression shifted—curiosity, cautious but open. “Understand what?” Reece searched for the right words. Failed. Tried anyway. “You’re… intentional. About how you look. About how you move. But it’s not… shallow.” Alex didn’t interrupt. “It’s like…” Reece exhaled softly. “Like you’re building something.” A long pause. Then Alex looked down at his hands, fingers lightly brushing the fabric at his wrist. “I am,” he said quietly.
