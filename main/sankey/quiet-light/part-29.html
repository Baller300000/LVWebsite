The room felt different that evening. Not tense. Structured. Alex had changed—comfortable, soft clothing again. The tail now part of that, subtle but present. He stood near the edge of the room, not quite sure where to position himself. Nicholas noticed immediately. “Come here,” he said, calm, steady. Alex moved closer. Not rushed. But with intent. “Sit,” Nicholas said. A pause. Then Alex lowered himself to the floor. Not exaggerated. Just… aligned with the instruction. Nicholas watched carefully. No judgment. Only observation. “Good,” he said. Simple. Clear. Alex felt it immediately—that small shift inside, the one he had tried to explain before. The quiet satisfaction of understanding what was expected. Nicholas reached for the small box, breaking one of the treats in half before offering it. Alex hesitated for a fraction of a second—then accepted it, not as a joke, not as something diminishing. As part of the structure they had agreed on. Nicholas noted the reaction. Relaxed posture. Slight lean forward. No tension. “Stay,” he said next. Alex remained still. Not rigid. Just present. Nicholas stepped back slightly, creating space. Watched. Counted silently. Then returned. “Good,” he said again. Another small reward.
