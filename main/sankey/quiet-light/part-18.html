Alex didn’t introduce it immediately. That part of himself. Not because he was hiding it. Because he needed to understand it first in the context of them. It came up on a quiet night. The kind where conversation drifts instead of moves. “I don’t always feel human,” Alex said, lying on his side, facing Reece. Reece didn’t react with confusion. “Explain.” Alex exhaled, staring at the ceiling. “It’s not literal. It’s… a feeling. Like the way people expect me to exist doesn’t match how I actually feel inside.” Reece nodded slightly. “That tracks.” Alex glanced at him, a small smile. “Of course you’d say that.” A pause. “Sometimes it feels easier to express myself through something simpler. Something… instinctive.” Reece stayed quiet. Listening. “I like softness. I like closeness. I like being… guided, sometimes.” Alex’s voice lowered slightly, not out of shame, but precision. “Not controlled like I don’t have agency. But… trusted enough to let go of it for a while.” Reece absorbed that. Mapped it. “Like shifting responsibility,” he said. Alex nodded. “Exactly.” A beat. “And sometimes that shows up as… puppy-like behavior.” Silence. Not uncomfortable. Just real. Reece asked the only relevant question. “What does that mean for you?”
