After that, things didn’t go back.

They moved forward.

Nicholas started asking more questions.

Not intrusive ones. Not rapid.

But intentional.

Alex answered. Sometimes directly. Sometimes not.

But he stayed.

Alex started letting the flickers show.

The moments where confidence dipped. Where certainty wavered. Where being “himself” wasn’t simple or clean.

Nicholas didn’t react.

He just… stayed.

One evening, as the sky turned dim and the courtyard emptied again, Alex leaned back against the stone wall.

“You know what scares me?” he asked.

Nicholas glanced at him. “What?”

“That if I stop performing even a little… people will leave.”

Nicholas didn’t answer immediately.

Then:

“I wouldn’t.”

Alex studied him.

“You don’t know that.”

Nicholas shook his head. “I do.”

“Why?”

Nicholas hesitated.

Then said it anyway.

“Because I like you when you’re not trying.”

A pause.

“And when you are.”

Alex looked away.

Not to hide.

To process.
