Quiet Light - Part 7 - Light

Nothing dramatic marked the shift.

No confession. No moment of clarity.

Just accumulation.

Shared time.

Shared silence.

Shared understanding.

One night, walking back from campus, the air cold and still, Alex brushed his hand against Nicholas’s.

This time, Nicholas didn’t pull away.

He didn’t react at all.

He just let it happen.

That was enough.
