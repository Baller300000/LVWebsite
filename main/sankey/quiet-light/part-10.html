Intimacy didn’t arrive as a single moment. It accumulated. It was in the way Alex started sitting closer without thinking. In the way Reece stopped noticing the distance at all. In the way their conversations shifted from observations to admissions. “I used to think I had to choose,” Alex said one night, voice quieter than usual. “Between being accepted and being… accurate.” Reece leaned back against the wall beside him. “And now?” Alex let out a slow breath. “Now I think neither one is guaranteed.” Reece turned slightly toward him. “So what do you choose?” Alex smiled faintly. “Myself.” A beat. “Most days.” Reece nodded. “That seems enough.” Alex looked at him. “It isn’t. Not always.” Reece didn’t argue. “I know,” he said. That was the difference. Reece didn’t try to fix things. He didn’t simplify them. He just… held space for them to exist.
