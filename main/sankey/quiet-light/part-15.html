“You’re not as fragile as people think,” Reece said quietly. They were sitting close again, shoulders touching, the familiarity no longer uncertain. Alex tilted his head. “And what do people think?” Reece considered. “That you’re soft.” Alex smiled faintly. “I am soft.” Reece shook his head slightly. “That’s not the same as fragile.” A pause. “You’re… deliberate,” Reece continued. “Everything you do. It’s not random. It’s not weak.” Alex watched him closely now. “You’ve thought about this.” Reece nodded once. “I think about you a lot.” Silence. But not the heavy kind. Something steadier.
