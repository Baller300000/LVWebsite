The first real break came from something small. It always does. Reece saw Alex across campus, standing with a group he didn’t recognize. Louder people. Sharper voices. The kind that filled space aggressively instead of naturally. Alex laughed at something one of them said. But it wasn’t the same laugh. Reece knew that immediately. He watched longer than he should have. One of them said something—too quiet to hear—but Alex’s shoulders stiffened just slightly before he brushed it off. Reece looked away. He told himself it wasn’t his place. Later, when Alex came to the fountain, something felt off. Reece could feel it in the way he sat. The way he didn’t quite settle. “You okay?” Reece asked. Alex blinked, surprised. “Yeah. Why?” “You seem… different.” A pause. Then Alex smiled. That same not-quite-right smile. “I’m always different.” Reece shook his head. “No. I mean—” He stopped. Words failed him. Alex’s expression shifted again. Sharper now. “What do you mean?” Reece hesitated too long. “Nothing,” he said. That was the wrong answer. Alex stood. “Right.” His voice wasn’t loud. It didn’t need to be. Reece felt the distance immediately. “I didn’t mean—” “I know,” Alex cut in. But he didn’t stay.
