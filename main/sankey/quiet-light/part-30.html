Quiet Light - Part 30 - Calibration

They didn’t do much more that first time.

It wasn’t about intensity.

It was about calibration.

“What worked?” Nicholas asked afterward.

Alex leaned back against the bed again, calmer now.

“The clarity,” he said. “Knowing exactly what you meant.”

A pause.

“And the tone. You didn’t hesitate.”

Nicholas nodded. “I was focusing on consistency.”

Alex smiled faintly.

“It showed.”

“What didn’t work?” Nicholas asked.

Alex thought for a moment.

“Nothing yet,” he said. Then, more carefully: “Just… don’t rush adding complexity.”

Nicholas nodded immediately.

“That aligns with what I was planning.”
