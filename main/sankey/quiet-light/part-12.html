Quiet Light - Part 12 - Misalignment

Growth doesn’t remove conflict.

It changes it.

The next fracture came from closeness, not distance.

Alex had grown used to being seen by Nicholas.

But being seen didn’t mean being understood completely.

It never does.

“You don’t ask about it,” Alex said one afternoon.

Nicholas looked up from his sketchbook. “About what?”

“This.”

Alex gestured vaguely at himself.

“At… everything.”

Nicholas frowned slightly. “I thought you didn’t want to be reduced to that.”

“I don’t.”

“Then—”

“But ignoring it isn’t the same as respecting it.”

Nicholas paused.

That landed harder than expected.

“I’m not ignoring it,” he said carefully. “I just don’t want to say something wrong.”

Alex’s expression softened, but only slightly.

“You will.”

Nicholas blinked. “What?”

“You’ll say something wrong. That’s how this works.”

A beat.

“I don’t need you to be perfect. I need you to be present.”

Nicholas looked down at his hands.

“That’s harder.”

“I know.”

Silence stretched.

Not empty.

But tense.

“I don’t always know what you need,” Nicholas said quietly.

Alex stepped closer.

“Then ask.”
