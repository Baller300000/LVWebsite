The night it shifted again, it was subtle. Like everything else. They were walking back from the far side of campus, the path dimly lit, the city distant but present in the background. Alex’s hand brushed Reece’s again. This time, it didn’t feel accidental. Reece noticed. Of course he did. He always did. But instead of waiting, instead of analyzing, instead of stepping back— He turned his hand slightly. Closed the distance. Not fully. Just enough. Alex stilled for half a second. Then relaxed into it. No words. None needed.
