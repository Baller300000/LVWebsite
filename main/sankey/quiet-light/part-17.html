They didn’t confess in a dramatic way. No single moment. No rehearsed words. It emerged the way everything else between them had—slow, cumulative, undeniable. “You’re already here most of the time,” Alex said one evening, sitting cross-legged on his bed, watching Reece stand awkwardly near the door. “You could just… stay.” Reece glanced at him. “I don’t want to assume.” Alex tilted his head, a faint smile forming. “You assume everything else. You analyze me like a system.” “That’s different.” “How?” Reece hesitated. Then, quieter: “This matters more.” That landed. Alex’s expression softened in a way it rarely did—unguarded, almost fragile. “Good,” he said. A pause. “Because it matters to me too.” Reece stepped closer. Not rushed. Not uncertain. Just… decided.
