When it ended, it didn’t break.

It softened.

Alex leaned back against the bed again, quieter now, but not distant.

Nicholas sat beside him.

Close.

Not touching at first.

“How was that?” Nicholas asked.

Alex smiled faintly, eyes half-lidded.

“Clear.”

A pause.

“Safe.”

Nicholas nodded.

“Anything to change?”

Alex thought about it.

“Not yet.”

He shifted slightly, leaning into Nicholas’s shoulder this time.

More familiar.

More human, in a different way.

“You didn’t overdo it,” Alex added.

Nicholas glanced at him. “That was the goal.”
