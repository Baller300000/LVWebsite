When it ended, it didn’t break. It softened. Alex leaned back against the bed again, quieter now, but not distant. Reece sat beside him. Close. Not touching at first. “How was that?” Reece asked. Alex smiled faintly, eyes half-lidded. “Clear.” A pause. “Safe.” Reece nodded. “Anything to change?” Alex thought about it. “Not yet.” He shifted slightly, leaning into Reece’s shoulder this time. More familiar. More human, in a different way. “You didn’t overdo it,” Alex added. Reece glanced at him. “That was the goal.”
