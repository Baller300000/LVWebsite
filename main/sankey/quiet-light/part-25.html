“You’re not less when you’re like that,” Nicholas said one night.

Alex looked at him, curious.

“Like what?”

“Softer. Simpler.”

Alex held his gaze.

“Some people would say I am.”

Nicholas shook his head.

“They’re wrong.”

A pause.

“You’re just… choosing a different way to exist for a while.”

Alex smiled.

Not wide.

But real.

“That’s exactly what it feels like.”
