The first time it happened, it was quiet. No build-up. No announcement. They were in Alex’s room. Late. Calm. Familiar. Alex sat on the floor, back against the bed, watching Reece read something he wasn’t fully focused on. There was a tension in him. Not anxiety. Anticipation. “Reece.” He looked up. “Yes?” A pause. Then, carefully: “Can we try it?” Reece didn’t move immediately. He evaluated. Not the request. The state behind it. Alex wasn’t uncertain. Just… open. “Tell me what you need,” Reece said. Alex shifted slightly, moving closer, settling on his knees instead of sitting. The movement was deliberate, but not exaggerated. “I need you to be… steady,” he said. “Clear. Not hesitant.” Reece closed the book. Set it aside. Then he adjusted his posture. Subtle changes. But intentional. “Okay,” he said. That was enough. Alex’s shoulders relaxed almost immediately. Like something had clicked into place. He leaned forward slightly, resting his head briefly against Reece’s knee. Testing. Not asking. Reece’s hand hovered for a second. Then lowered. Resting gently against Alex’s hair. No rush. No force. Just contact. Alex exhaled. A soft, quiet sound. Not performative. Relief.
